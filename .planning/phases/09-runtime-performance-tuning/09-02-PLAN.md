<objective>
RPERF-02 + RPERF-04 (combobox). Reduce filter-per-keystroke work behavior-
preservingly by memoizing the redundant filtering, and guard the tuning with an
accessible-name/role snapshot. Expands off the measurement spine proven in Plan 01
(reuses harness.countMethod).

Today `filterOptions(this._allOptions, this.value, this.remote)` runs 4–5× per
keystroke across five call sites (render 938, ListboxNav getOptions 196, keydown
704, count getter 795, select-mode 893), and `_allOptions` re-spreads
`[...this.options, ...this._slottedOptions]` on every call. Per D-01 the fix is
MEMOIZE ONLY — compute the filtered list once per (options-identity,
slotted-identity, value, remote) and thread it through all sites; NEVER debounce
(that would shift the observable am-search cadence and when the list visibly
updates — behavior the frozen surface must preserve).

Purpose: cut per-keystroke main-thread work on the heaviest form input. Output:
two-level identity memo behind a nameable _computeFilteredOptions, filterCalls
count key, and a report-only combobox a11y snapshot guard.
</objective>
