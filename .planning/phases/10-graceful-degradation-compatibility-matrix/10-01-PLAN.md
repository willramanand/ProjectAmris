<objective>
TRACER plan for Phase 10 (COMPAT-01 + COMPAT-02 core). Prove the entire
graceful-degradation seam end-to-end on ONE representative component
(am-input) before the pattern expands to the other 15 form-associated
components in Plans 04/05: build `capabilities.ts` (four independently
memoized probes mirroring the `lazy-load.ts` `??=` probe-once idiom), build
the `attachInternalsSafe()` guarded-attach helper on top of it, wire it into
`input.ts`, and prove — via a jsdom capability-off test — that the component
constructs, connects, and renders without throwing when ElementInternals form
association is forced absent, while staying byte-identical above the floor.

Per RESEARCH.md: 16 `attachInternals()` call sites across 15 files call the
raw, unguarded API in their constructors, each of which throws below Safari
16.4. This plan builds the guard (the chokepoint every later component reuses)
and validates it against `am-input`, the best-understood component with both a
`setFormValue` and two `setValidity` call sites plus a `ValidationController`
wiring that must stay null-tolerant.

Purpose: prove the capability-probing + guarded-attach architecture on the
hardest single component (full validation wiring) before the mechanical
rollout, so any architectural problem surfaces on one file, not sixteen.
Output: `capabilities.ts`, `attach-internals-safe.ts`, `am-input` wired
null-safely, and the jsdom test harness (independent-probe assertions +
capability-off constructor proof) the rollout plans extend.
</objective>
